Interactive Dashboard: University Students’ Social Media Habits

The purpose of my interactive dashboard is to explore university students’ social media habits and their possible relationships with sleep, mental health, and academic experiences. This dashboard is part of my capstone project, Healthy Habits in a Digital World: Promoting Balanced Technology Use. I created this dashboard using Google Looker Studio and a Kaggle dataset called Students Social Media Addiction. After removing high school records, I used 678 undergraduate and graduate student records. I designed five charts to show average daily social media usage, sleep hours, mental health scores, reported academic impact, and addiction scores. I also added four interactive filters for academic level, gender, country, and most-used platform. These filters allow users to explore different student groups. The simple layout, clear chart titles, summary cards, and hover tooltips make the information easier to understand. This dashboard connects to my research question by helping students recognize possible patterns in their digital habits. The results support my awareness campaign's message about mindful scrolling and balanced technology use. However, the dataset has limited documentation, so the findings are exploratory and do not establish cause and effect.
