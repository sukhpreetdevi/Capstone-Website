Description explaining purpose, design choices, and connection to research

My Digital Balance is an interactive mobile app prototype created in Figma for my capstone project, Healthy Habits in a Digital World: Promoting Balanced Technology Use. Its purpose is to help university students become more aware of their recreational screen time and develop healthier digital habits through small, realistic choices. The prototype includes a welcome screen, simple starting steps, a dashboard, personal screen-time goals, break reminders, daily reflection questions, and a weekly progress chart. The chart and dashboard use illustrative data to demonstrate how the app could work. These features encourage students to notice their habits, set optional goals, and reflect without feeling judged. For the design, I used soft green and white colours, readable text, clear headings, simple icons, and rounded buttons. The iPhone layout and straightforward navigation make the prototype easy to explore. The consistent layout also helps students move between features easily, while supportive messages encourage positive choices without creating unnecessary pressure. This deliverable connects to my capstone research question by translating screen-time awareness messages into practical, student-friendly interactions. Rather than treating all technology use as harmful, it promotes intentional recreational screen time and a healthier balance between online and offline activities.
