Research and healthy digital habits

Research Questions

How can a targeted social media awareness campaign encourage healthier recreational screen-time habits among university students at the University of Niagara Falls Canada, based on research published between 2024 and 2026?

Three types of technology use

Ask what the screen is doing for you.

Academic: lectures, readings, research, assignments, and collaboration.

Essential: banking, communication, health care, navigation, and administration.

Recreational: streaming, gaming, scrolling, browsing, and digital leisure.

Research stance

Associations are signals, not diagnoses.

Research can show that two patterns occur together. It cannot automatically prove that one caused the other.
